Bilkent University · CS 588 Data Science for Software Engineering · term project, Fall 2025–26

Graph-Enhanced Transformer for Duplicate Bug Report Detection

Semi-supervised: every report joins the graph, labelled or not

Kerem Bayramoğlu · Hüseyin Karaca · Emirhan Koç · Hasan Erkin Ünlü · Rabia Ela Ünlü

Authors in alphabetical order by surname · Bilkent University

Transformer encoders match bug reports well but learn only from the few pairs a triager has already marked as duplicates. We put the whole corpus into one title-similarity graph and let a two-layer GCN carry that supervision to the reports no pair ever touches. On Eclipse and Thunderbird it ties the transformer baselines for 10–12% more training time.
